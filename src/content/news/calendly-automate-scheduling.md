Arranging meetings by e-mail takes time, and every lost hour costs consultants, coaches and freelancers money. [Calendly](https://calendly.com/) solves this: clients choose a convenient time themselves, and the meeting appears in your calendar automatically.

## What Calendly can do

- **Sync with your calendars**, so you are never double-booked.
- **Send automatic reminders** to you and your clients.
- **Connect to video and payment platforms** — the meeting link and the payment are part of the booking.
- **Let clients book directly** from your website, e-mail signature or social media profile.
