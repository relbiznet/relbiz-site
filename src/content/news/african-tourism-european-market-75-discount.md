For **$20** — 25% of our standard advertising rate — your business receives a promotional listing that includes:

- an article about your business of up to 5,000 characters;

Tour operators, travel agencies, safari camps, lodges, hotels and guides from African countries that want to attract guests and partners from Europe.

The offer runs from **15 December 2024 to 15 January 2025**, and the number of places is limited. Write to [info@relbiz.net](mailto:info@relbiz.net) to reserve your listing.
