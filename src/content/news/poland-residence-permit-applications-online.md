Foreign entrepreneurs and professionals who plan to settle in Poland now apply for residence through one digital channel. Since **27 April 2026**, applications for temporary residence, permanent residence and EU long-term resident status can only be submitted through **MOS** (Moduł Obsługi Spraw), the online portal of Poland's Office for Foreigners: [mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl](https://mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl/).

## What this means in practice

- **No more paper.** Applications are filed, tracked and managed online. Paper applications that did not reach a voivodeship office before the deadline are not processed.
- **Prepare digitally.** You need a MOS account, an electronic signature and scanned supporting documents. For temporary residence with work, the employer completes its part of the application in the same system.
- **Check the exceptions.** A few categories of applicants still file on paper, so confirm your case before you apply.

## Why it matters for business

For companies from Africa and other regions entering the Polish and wider EU market, this is good news: a clearer process and less dependence on queues at regional offices. The first application, though, has to be set up correctly.
