**You are not alone.** If you are preparing for mediation, you may feel frightened, angry, exhausted or hopeful. Most books about mediation are written for mediators. *Your Mediation Journey* by Dr. Emmy Irobi is written for the people who actually sit at the table.

The book and its companion workbook now have their own website: [mfe.relbiz.net](https://mfe.relbiz.net/).

## The book: How People Grow Through Conflict

A warm, practical guide to what mediation is, what happens in a session and how to prepare for it. The book introduces the five levels of conflict and includes stories based on real mediations.

## The Companion Workbook

30 guided exercises, checklists and journal pages that help you prepare before your mediation, reflect during it and move forward after it.

## For mediators, trainers and universities

- **Mediators** can give the book to clients before the first session.
- **Trainers and universities** can use it as a study text.
- **Bulk orders** of printed copies and **online talks by the author** are available.

Both paperbacks, with a look inside the pages, are on the new website: [mfe.relbiz.net](https://mfe.relbiz.net/).
