RelBiz.NET started as a European Union project within the East Invest programme. Today we use that experience to help African companies present themselves to European buyers and partners.

## Our marketing services

- **Google Ads campaigns** — search and display advertising that brings European customers to your website.
- **Facebook and Instagram advertising** — targeted campaigns for the audiences and countries you want to reach.
- **Chatbots and auto-responders** — so that no inquiry from a potential partner stays unanswered, whatever the time zone.
- **Systematic marketing strategy** — positioning, messages and a plan of activities for entering a new market.

Every project is tailored to the goals of the company and the market it is entering. [Contact us](/contacts/) to discuss yours.
